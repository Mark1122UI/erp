# Universal ERP — User & Cashier Operational Manual

This guide explains how to use Universal ERP for everyday retail, sales, inventory, and point-of-sale operations.

---

## 1. Getting Started & Logging In

1. Open your web browser and navigate to your store URL (e.g. `https://erp.yourstore.com`).
2. Enter your work email and password.
3. Click **Log In**. You will land on your store Dashboard or POS Register.

---

## 2. Using the Point of Sale (POS)

### Selling Products by Barcode Scan
1. Connect a USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner (or enable the camera scanner on mobile/tablet).
2. Scan the barcode on the item packaging. The item will automatically appear in your active sale cart.
3. To increase quantity, scan the item again or click the `+` button in the cart.

### Searching Products Manually
1. Type the product name or SKU into the POS search bar.
2. Click or tap the product card to add it to the cart.

### Checkout & Payment
1. Select the customer (or leave as Walk-in Customer).
2. Click **Checkout**.
3. Choose the payment method:
   - **Cash**: Enter the amount received. The system will calculate the change to give the customer.
   - **Card / Electronic**: Confirm payment processing.
4. Click **Complete Sale**.
5. The cash drawer opens (if configured) and the thermal receipt displays for printing.

---

## 3. Working Offline During Internet Outages

1. If your internet connection drops, the POS indicator will display **Offline Mode (Active)**.
2. Continue scanning items and completing sales as normal. Sales are safely stored on your device.
3. Once internet connectivity is restored, the POS will automatically synchronize queued offline sales with the server in the background.

---

## 4. Processing Customer Returns

1. Go to **Sales $\rightarrow$ Returns**.
2. Click **New Return**.
3. Enter the original Invoice / Receipt number.
4. Select the items being returned and the refund method (Cash / Account Credit).
5. Confirm the return. The inventory will automatically be restocked to your store shelf.

---

## 5. Checking Stock Levels

1. Go to **Inventory $\rightarrow$ Stock Levels**.
2. Search for any product or filter by store location.
3. The table displays available units, incoming stock, and low-stock indicators.
